How to Convert HEIC to JPG for Free (No App Needed)

Image Tools • 4 min read • Updated 29 Aug 2026 • iPhone photos

iPhone saves photos as HEIC. Windows, Android and many sites still ask for JPG. Convert in seconds — no app, no signup, no upload. Files stay private on your device and work offline.

Why iPhone uses HEIC

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container, .heic/.heif) keeps quality with ~50% smaller files than JPG. Apple uses it by default since iOS 11. Problem: Windows, older Android, email and many websites don’t open HEIC without converting to JPG.

Steps — convert HEIC to JPG in browser

  1. Open the converter: Go to HEIC to JPG (works on iPhone, Android, Mac, Windows).
  2. Drop your HEICs: Drag & drop or tap Browse files. Select one or many .heic/.heif files. Previews appear instantly.
  3. Choose quality: Keep 90% for photos (recommended). Lower for smaller files, 100% for max fidelity. Optionally tick Resize and set width/height — aspect ratio stays locked.
  4. Convert & download: Tap Convert to JPG → Download per file or Download All JPGs. Files are white-background JPGs, universal everywhere.
Private by design: Your HEIC is decoded with heic2any in your browser, drawn to a canvas and exported as JPG. No upload, no storage — works offline after load.

Tips to keep quality

  • Use 90–95% quality — looks identical to HEIC, smaller than 100%.
  • Don’t re-save the same JPG many times — each save adds loss. Keep the HEIC as master.
  • For web, consider Image to WebP after converting — even smaller than JPG.

FAQ

Is it really free without an app?

Yes — open HEIC to JPG in your browser, drop files, download. No install, no account, no watermark.

Are my iPhone photos private?

Yes. Decoding and conversion run locally via canvas. Files never leave your device.

Can I convert many HEICs at once?

Yes — select many .heic/.heif files, convert in one click, download individually or “Download All JPGs”.

HEIC vs JPG size?

A HEIC is ~50% smaller than the same photo as JPG. Converted JPG at 90% will be larger than the HEIC, but universal and still smaller than PNG.
