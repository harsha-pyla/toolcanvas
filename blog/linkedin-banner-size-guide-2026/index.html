LinkedIn Banner Size Guide (2026)

Social Media • 4 min read • Updated 29 Aug 2026 • LinkedIn sizes

Your LinkedIn banner is cropped differently on mobile, desktop and company pages. Use these exact 2026 pixels — 1584×396 for personal, 1128×191 for company — plus profile and post sizes. Then resize and preview privately (no upload).

Do this: Create at 1584×396, keep key text/logo inside center 1000×180 safe area. Preview before you upload.

Steps — perfect banner in 30s

  1. Resize exact: Open Image Resizer → enter 1584 × 396 (or 1128×191 for company) → keep Maintain Aspect Ratio off for exact.
  2. Keep safe: Place logo/text center. Avoid top 30px and side 200px — those get cropped on mobile.
  3. Export: JPG 85–90% for photos, PNG for graphics with text. Max 8MB.
  4. Preview: Open LinkedIn Profile Preview → upload cover + profile → check circle crop and banner safe area on both views.
  5. Fix & re-export: If text is cut, move it inward and re-export.

Common mistakes

  • Using 1584×396 for company page — company needs 1128×191, much flatter.
  • Putting text at edges — mobile crops sides. Keep center.
  • Uploading < 1200px wide for posts — looks blurry. Use 1200×627 minimum.
  • Using PNG for photo banners — PNG is larger; JPG 90% is sharper and smaller.

FAQ

Why does my banner look blurry?

You uploaded smaller than 1584×396. LinkedIn upscales and softens. Upload exact 1584×396 at JPG 90%.

Company vs personal banner?

Personal 1584×396, company 1128×191. They are not interchangeable — company is ~30% shorter.

Is preview private?

Yes — all ToolCanvas previews run locally via canvas and FileReader. No upload, works offline.
