How to get it right every time

  1. Start from larger: Upload the biggest original you have. Downscaling keeps sharpness; upscaling blurs.
  2. Resize exact: In Image Resizer enter Width × Height from table, keep Maintain Aspect Ratio off for exact, or on to avoid stretch.
  3. Export right: Photo → JPG 85–90% or WebP 80%; graphic with text → PNG. See PNG vs JPG vs WebP.
  4. Preview before posting: Check crop:
    • Instagram grid/bio → Instagram Profile Preview
    • LinkedIn banner/profile → LinkedIn Profile Preview
    • Business card/QR → Social Media Card

Quick presets — copy these

FAQ

What if I upload a larger image?

Platforms compress and crop. Uploading exact size from the table keeps it sharp and avoids auto-crop cutting text.

JPG or PNG for these sizes?

Photo → JPG 85–90% or WebP 80% (smaller). Graphics with text → PNG. See our compress guide.

Is resizing private?

Yes — all ToolCanvas image tools run locally via canvas. No upload, works offline after load.
