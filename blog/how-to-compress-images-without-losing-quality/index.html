How to Compress Images Without Losing Quality

Image Tools • 4 min read • Updated 29 Aug 2026 • Compression

Shrink images without making them blurry. Use resizing, the right format and 90% JPG or WebP — free, private, no upload. Files stay on your device and work offline.

Fast rule: Don’t just lower quality. Resize first, then export at JPG 85–90% or WebP. You’ll cut 50–70% size with no visible loss.

Why images get large

A phone photo is often 4000×3000 px, 5–8 MB. For web, email or slides you rarely need that many pixels. Extra pixels = extra bytes. Compression works by removing pixels (resize) and by smart encoding (JPG/WebP quality).

Steps — compress without visible loss

  1. Resize to needed size: Open Image Resizer. If an image is 4000 px wide but your page shows 1200 px, resize to 1200 (keep aspect ratio). This alone cuts ~70% size.
  2. Pick the right format:
    • Photo → JPG or WebP
    • Graphic/logo with sharp edges or transparency → PNG or WebP
    Need help? See PNG vs JPG vs WebP.
  3. Export at the right quality: In Image to JPG or Image to WebP choose 85–90% for JPG, 80–85% for WebP. At 90% you keep detail but drop ~40% bytes vs 100%.
  4. Download & check: Compare zoomed 100% — edges stay sharp, no blocks. If file still large, try WebP or resize a bit more.

Lossy vs lossless — plain terms

MethodHow it savesQualityWhen to use
Resize (fewer pixels)Removes pixelsNo loss per pixel, just smallerAlways first step if image is too large
JPG quality 90%Discards invisible detailVisually losslessPhotos for web/email
WebP 80%Smarter encodingVisually losslessSmallest for web with transparency
PNGLosslessPerfectKeep for editing, not for smallest web
Tip: One resize + WebP at 80% often beats two quality drops. Resize first, then choose format/quality once.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Saving the same JPG again and again at low quality — each save adds loss. Keep a master PNG/JPG at 90%+ and export copies.
  • Upscaling a small image — makes it blurry, not sharper. Only downscale.
  • Using PNG for photos — PNG is larger than JPG/WebP for photos. Use PNG only for graphics/screenshots.

FAQ

Will 90% look worse?

At normal viewing distance, no. At 100% zoom, 90% keeps edges sharp and removes only invisible noise. 85–90% is the sweet spot for photos.

Can I compress without resizing?

Yes — just convert at 90% JPG or 80% WebP. You’ll still save ~30–40% with no visible loss.

Is it private?

Yes. Images are processed locally via canvas. No upload, works offline after load.
