Privacy Policy

At ToolCanvas (https://toolcanvas.online/), we believe online text utilities should be powerful, effortless, and fundamentally respectful of your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we handle information, what we do (and do not) collect, and the measures we take to keep your experience secure and private.

1. Our Core Privacy Principles

ToolCanvas is designed from the ground up as a privacy-conscious utility platform. We do not require account registration, we do not require your email address or personal credentials to use our tools, and we do not create personal tracking profiles about you.

2. Your Text Inputs and Tool Processing

Our online tools—including the Case Converter, Word & Character Counter, Duplicate Line Remover, Text Cleaner, and Text Compare—are built for direct, in-session utility.

When you enter, paste, edit, or analyze text in any ToolCanvas tool:

  • Zero Text Retention: We do not store, copy, record, or retain the content you input or the results produced.
  • No Server Uploads: Your input text is never saved to our web servers, external databases, or third-party storage services.
  • Session Privacy: Once you clear your input, navigate away, or close your browser tab, your text data is gone from your session. We have no access to it, and no history is preserved on our systems.

3. Diagnostic and Network Log Data

Like almost all modern websites, our hosting servers automatically log standard network communication requests when you access pages on ToolCanvas. This diagnostic information is strictly non-identifying and includes:

  • Your Internet Protocol (IP) address (used for server routing, DDoS mitigation, and approximate geographic location)
  • Browser type, software version, and operating system
  • Date, time stamp, and duration of page requests
  • Referring web addresses and page navigation paths

We use this data exclusively to keep our infrastructure secure, prevent malicious automated attacks, diagnose server faults, and monitor general traffic patterns. We never combine network log entries with personal identity records.

4. Cookies and Site Preferences

ToolCanvas uses cookies and browser storage solely to deliver essential functionality and enhance your browsing experience.

Specifically, we use storage to remember your chosen visual theme (Dark Mode or Light Mode) so your preference remains consistent across pages and repeat visits. We do not use first-party cookies to track you across external websites.

You are free to configure your web browser to block or clear cookies at any time. If you disable cookies, all ToolCanvas text utilities will continue to function normally, though visual preferences will reset to default when you return.

5. Advertising Partners and Google AdSense

To maintain ToolCanvas as a completely free service without paid subscriptions, we partner with third-party advertising providers, including Google AdSense, to display non-intrusive advertisements on our site.

Third-party ad vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to our website or other sites on the Internet. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve relevant advertisements to users based on browsing activity.

Managing Ad Preferences: You may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising through the Digital Advertising Alliance Consumer Choice Tool or the Network Advertising Initiative.

6. European Privacy Rights (GDPR & UK GDPR)

If you are accessing ToolCanvas from within the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you enjoy specific legal rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Where required, our website provides a certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) allowing you to freely grant, refuse, or customize consent for non-essential cookies and personalized ad processing. You may reopen and update your privacy choices at any time.

Under GDPR, you also hold rights to access, rectify, or request deletion of any personal data we may hold, to restrict or object to certain processing activities, and to submit complaints to your local data protection authority.

7. California and US State Privacy Disclosures (CCPA & CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have rights regarding their personal information:

  • No Sale of Personal Data: ToolCanvas does not sell personal information or share personal data with third-party data brokers.
  • Right to Know and Delete: You have the right to request disclosure of what personal information is collected and request its deletion.
  • Non-Discrimination: We do not discriminate against any user for exercising their privacy rights.

8. Security and Data Protection

We take appropriate physical, administrative, and technological precautions to safeguard our hosting infrastructure and protect information from loss, theft, and unauthorized access or modification. However, because no internet transmission or electronic storage method can guarantee 100% absolute security, we encourage users to practice responsible digital hygiene.

9. Links to External Websites

ToolCanvas may contain informative links to external websites, articles, or resources. We do not operate or control third-party websites, and we are not responsible for their respective privacy practices, terms, or content. We encourage you to inspect the privacy notices of any external site you visit.

10. Children’s Online Privacy

ToolCanvas does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a minor has provided personal details through our website, please notify us immediately so we can promptly take appropriate corrective action.

11. Updates and Policy Revisions

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational improvements, tool enhancements, or evolving legal requirements. Whenever revisions occur, the updated policy will be posted on this page with the revised effective date shown below.

12. Contacting Us About Privacy

If you have questions, feedback, or inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy or our data handling practices, please contact us via our About & Contact page.
