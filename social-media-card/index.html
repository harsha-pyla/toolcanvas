How it works

Fill the form on the left or edit the hidden inputs — the bordered preview on the right updates instantly, debounced by about 80 ms, with no motion flourish. Upload an avatar via the small dashed dropzone, set contact rows, pick a preset or custom accent, and set orientation.

Add up to five social links and watch icons appear under the contact list. The QR canvas renders flat ink on white with a thin border and encodes your current website or a shareable card URL. Toggle Light or Dark at the top to audit the frame background and text contrast.

All generation stays in the browser. Download exports the exact preview frame at 2× resolution.

What you get

  • Print-ready visiting card with 1px bordered frame, no shadow or gradient
  • Social link bar with five-platform limit and inline remove controls
  • Flat QR code in ink-on-white with thin border and high error correction
  • Landscape and portrait layouts with three typography choices
  • Shareable URL and high-fidelity PNG export from local canvas
