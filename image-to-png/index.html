How it works

  1. Drag & drop JPG/WebP images or browse — no upload, local only.
  2. Optionally enable resize and set target pixels with aspect lock.
  3. Click Convert All Images to re-encode to lossless PNG.
  4. Download each PNG or all at once, then convert another batch.

What you get

  • Lossless PNG output with full alpha transparency preserved
  • Sharp text and edges — ideal for logos, screenshots, and graphics
  • Optional pixel resize with crisp canvas resampling
  • Batch processing with per-file thumbnails and status
