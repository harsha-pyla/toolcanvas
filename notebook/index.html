How it works

Focus on writing — the paper adapts to you. Choose ruled lines on ivory, yellow or white, graph grid for structured lists, plain white for clean copy or dark charcoal for night writing. Lines are drawn in the line colour over paper and stay aligned to your font size and line height.

Formatting stays local: font family, size and paper choice are saved to your browser. Autosave runs on every keystroke. Find and replace, undo and redo, print and download to .txt or styled .html all work offline after the first load.

Use the menu bar or the floating toolbar to switch fonts, insert the current date or pull a special character. Toggle full-screen for distraction-free drafting. All processing is client-side — your notes never leave your device.

What you get

  • ✓ Ruled lines and graph grid in line colour over paper, toggleable between Lined and Grid
  • ✓ Four font families, seven sizes (14–32 px) with dynamically aligned baselines
  • ✓ Find and replace, cut/copy/paste, local autosave, print and TXT/HTML export
  • ✓ Floating toolbar on desktop (top) and mobile (bottom) with grouped controls and crop-mark active states
  • ✓ Offline-first, spell highlighting with suggestions, special characters and date insertion
