How it works

Use the form on the left to set your cover, avatar, headline, location and connections, or click any text in the preview to edit directly. The right panel shows a bordered card that mirrors LinkedIn without device chrome.

Update the talks-about tags, company and school in the form and see the header row flip immediately. Edit the About paragraph and add experience or education entries — each entry keeps an editable logo, title, company, dates and description.

Switch Light and Dark at the top to audit contrast, and toggle Owner vs Visitor to preview the action buttons. Export uses local canvas capture.

What you get

  • Cover banner and avatar dropzones with local preview and placeholder handling
  • Headline, pronouns, location and connection line with inline editing
  • Premium badge toggle that persists and respects view mode
  • Experience and education lists with logo uploads and delete controls
  • Light, dark, owner and visitor states with instant, debounced rendering
