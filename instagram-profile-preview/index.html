How it works

Fill the form on the left or click any text, number or image inside the preview to edit directly. The preview uses a plain bordered frame so you see the real layout without device chrome.

Upload a square avatar, set your username, display name, category and bio. Adjust posts, followers and following to match your metrics, then build highlights and a six-tile grid. Drag tiles to reorder. Toggle Light or Dark at the top of the form to check contrast.

Everything renders locally in the browser. Use Download Mockup to export a PNG that matches the preview exactly.

What you get

  • Accurate bio, stats and verified-badge layout with live line breaks and links
  • Story highlights with editable titles and image covers plus delete and add
  • Six-photo feed grid with drag-to-reorder and per-tile replace
  • Light and dark theme that flips the preview frame instantly
  • High-resolution PNG export via local canvas, no upload
