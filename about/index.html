About ToolCanvas

ToolCanvas (https://toolcanvas.online/) is a free, straightforward collection of text utilities designed to make formatting, counting, and cleaning text fast and simple.

Why ToolCanvas was built

If you write articles, work with spreadsheets, write code, or edit text daily, you constantly run into small but repetitive text tasks. You might need to change a headline from uppercase to Title Case, strip duplicate rows from an email list, clean up messy whitespace and extra line breaks from a copied PDF, count words for an essay, or compare two versions of a draft to see what changed.

Many websites that offer these tools are slow to load, covered in popup ads, or try to push paid subscriptions for simple text operations. ToolCanvas was built to be a fast, clean alternative: a website that opens instantly, lets you paste your text, gives you the result right away, and lets you get back to your work.

Privacy and how your text is handled

Your text is private. When you use any tool on ToolCanvas, all calculations and text formatting happen directly in your web browser. We do not store, log, or upload the text you enter to our servers.

Once you clear your text or close your browser tab, that data is gone from your session. You do not need to create an account, log in, or give us your email address to use any of our tools.

Our tools

ToolCanvas currently includes five focused utilities:

  • Case Converter: Converts text between 11 standard formats, including Sentence case, lower case, UPPER CASE, Title Case, Capitalized Case, alternating case, camelCase, PascalCase, kebab-case, and snake_case.
  • Word & Character Counter: Gives you real-time counts for words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences, paragraphs, and estimates reading and speaking time as you type.
  • Duplicate Line Remover: Cleans up lists and data exports by removing repeated lines, with options to ignore case, trim spaces, and sort alphabetically.
  • Text Cleaner: Strips unwanted spaces, blank lines, tabs, and HTML tags, or normalizes line endings and quotes.
  • Text Compare: Lets you paste two pieces of text side by side to see additions, deletions, and differences highlighted clearly.

How the website is funded
