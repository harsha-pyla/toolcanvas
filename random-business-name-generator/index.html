Instantly generate professional, unique business name ideas for your startup, company, or side project. Select your industry, customize naming styles, and optionally include matching slogans.

    How it works

    Choose your industry and preferred naming style, set how many ideas you need, and optionally tick “Include Slogan”. Press Generate Names and the tool combines curated roots, professional suffixes, and descriptive syllables tailored to your industry — all locally in your browser. No data leaves your device.

    Use the industry filter to bias vocabulary toward tech, creative, finance, wellness, or retail, or pick “Surprise Me” for a mixed set. Adjust the style to explore modern, classic, abstract, or compound constructions.

    What you get

    • Memorable brand names — built from industry-matched roots and suffixes, deduplicated per batch.
    • Optional slogans — context-appropriate taglines that help you visualise the brand.
    • Industry & style chips — coloured left-edge tags that show the source of each idea.
    • Per-item copy + bulk actions — copy any single idea or copy all / download as a clean text file.
    • Private generation — runs fully offline after page load; nothing is uploaded.
