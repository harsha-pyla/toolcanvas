How HEIC to JPG Works — Private & Offline

Your HEIC file is decoded in your browser with heic2any and drawn to a canvas, then exported as JPG with your chosen quality. No upload — everything stays on your device and works offline after load.

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is the iPhone default. JPG is universal — every browser, app and printer opens it.

Why Convert HEIC to JPG?

  • Universal compatibility: JPG opens everywhere — Windows, Android, web, email. HEIC often fails outside Apple.
  • Easy sharing: Social apps and older devices handle JPG without conversion prompts.
  • Choose quality: 90% keeps photo detail small. Lower for web, 100% for max fidelity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my HEIC private?

Yes. Files never leave your device. No server upload, no storage.

Will quality drop?

JPG is lossy. At 90% you’ll rarely see loss. Use 90–95% for photos. Avoid re-saving the same JPG many times.

Can I convert many HEICs at once?

Yes — drop multiple HEIC/HEIF files, convert together, download individually or via “Download All JPGs”.

Does it work offline?

After the page loads (and heic2any loads once), it works offline — all decoding is local.
