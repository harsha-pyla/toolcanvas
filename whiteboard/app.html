About ToolCanvas Whiteboard

What is the Infinite Whiteboard?

An unrestricted visual workspace— draw lines, sketch diagrams, write notes and pan across an infinite area with no borders. Auto-scales with zoom and pan.

Why is it secure?

Every stroke and setting is calculated locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded unless you create a share link.
