Blackboard — Infinite Dark Canvas

Chalk-textured infinite board for teaching and sketching. High-contrast dark surface, pastel chalk colours and full pan/zoom. Private by design.

Canvas · Board

Teach and sketch on a dark slate

Chalk pastels on a flat #1A1D1B board. Infinite pan/zoom, short share links and local autosave. Ideal for maths and diagrams.

Launch Blackboard →

Green · Charcoal · Navy themes · Pastel chalk set · Offline

How it works

Open the blackboard and write immediately. The surface is a flat dark slate — easier on the eyes for long teaching sessions and gives pastel chalk colours natural contrast.

Use Pen for bold chalk, Pencil for fine graphite and the stroke-eraser to delete a whole line in one swipe. Pick from white, yellow, blue, pink and mint pastels or add a custom colour, and adjust thickness with the slider. Toggle Plain, Grid or Dots backgrounds for graph work.

Everything is calculated locally: pan, zoom, strokes and settings never leave your browser. Share creates a short link, and Export saves a PNG cropped to your drawing. Switch themes between Chalkboard green, Charcoal and Navy without losing your strokes.

What you get

  • ✓ Flat #1A1D1B board with pastel chalk palette tuned for contrast against dark
  • ✓ Infinite canvas with 10–500% zoom, Space+drag panning and a recenter arrow
  • ✓ Pen, Pencil, stroke-eraser, size control, three board themes and Grid/Dots toggles
  • ✓ Local autosave, recent boards, undo/redo and PNG export on the dark surface
  • ✓ Short share links, stylus and touch support, keyboard shortcuts
