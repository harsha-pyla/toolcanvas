How it works

  1. Drop an image or browse — loaded into a local canvas buffer.
  2. Choose resize mode: pixels or percentage, with aspect lock.
  3. Select output format (PNG/JPEG/WebP) and quality for lossy types.
  4. Click Resize Image to render, then download the result.

What you get

  • High-quality bilinear interpolation resizing with exact pixel control
  • Percentage scaling and aspect-lock to prevent stretching
  • Choice of PNG, JPEG (white background), or WebP output
  • Size comparison with mono-tabular savings and instant download
